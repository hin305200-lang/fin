§ 1 Subject Matter of the Credit Request

(1) The subject matter of this Agreement is the Client’s request that the Manager add credit to the Client’s trading account, and the Manager’s acceptance of that request in accordance with this Agreement.

(2) The Credit Amount stated in Schedule 1 shall be added to the Client’s trading account. It is not paid out to the Client as a separate cash transfer.

(3) The credit is provided by NN Finanz and NN Group. NN Finanz (Marketplace) is a German financial platform (NN Finanz Website) that serves as a marketplace connecting savers with partner banks and credit unions. It provides access to high-yield call money (Tagesgeld), fixed-term deposits (Festgeld), and simple ETF portfolios. NN Group is a major international financial services company focused on insurance, pensions, and investments.

§ 2 Minimum Balance to Request Credit

(1) A credit request may be made only where the balance of the Client’s trading account is at least EUR 2,500.00 (in words: two thousand five hundred euros).

(2) Where the trading account balance is below EUR 2,500.00, this credit request shall not be accepted and no credit shall be added.

(3) The trading account balance stated in Schedule 1 is the balance on which this request is made. The Client confirms that this balance is not less than the minimum required under this § 2.

§ 3 Minimum Credit to Add

(1) The minimum credit to be added to the trading account shall be EUR 1,000.00 (in words: one thousand euros).

(2) A credit request below this minimum amount shall not be accepted.

§ 4 Addition of Credit

(1) Upon execution of this Agreement, and provided the conditions in § 2 and § 3 are met, the Manager shall add the Credit Amount provided by NN Finanz and NN Group to the Client’s trading account.

(2) The credit shall be deemed added only once the Credit Amount has been credited to the trading account.

(3) The credit is added within the framework of the operational cooperation between the Manager and [Name of Broker / Cooperation Partner] (hereinafter the “Broker”).

§ 5 Trading Account

(1) The credit shall be added solely to the trading account held for the Client with the Broker.

(2) The Client hereby authorizes the Broker, to the extent permitted by the applicable arrangements, to record the Credit Amount on the trading account and to apply it to trading activity carried out in the name and for the account of the Client.

(3) The credit remains on the trading account until 30 September 2026. No profit shall be withdrawn until the Credit Amount has first been paid out in full.

§ 6 Term

(1) This Agreement shall commence on execution. The credit shall remain added to the trading account until 30 September 2026.

(2) The Credit Amount shall be paid out in full on or before 30 September 2026. Ordinary termination before that date shall be excluded.

(3) The right of either Party to terminate this Agreement for good cause with immediate effect shall remain unaffected, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

§ 7 Payout and Withdrawal of Profit

(1) The Credit Amount may be used only within the Client’s trading account and only for trading carried out through the Broker.

(2) The credit shall be paid out in full before any profit is withdrawn from the trading account. No profit shall be paid or withdrawn while any part of the Credit Amount remains outstanding.

(3) The Manager shall confirm to the Client once the credit has been added to the trading account, and again once the credit has been paid out, stating the Credit Amount and the relevant date.

§ 8 Manager’s Obligations

The Manager shall, in particular:

(a) accept this credit request only where the trading account balance is at least EUR 2,500.00 and the Credit Amount is at least EUR 1,000.00;

(b) add the agreed Credit Amount to the Client’s trading account;

(c) withhold any withdrawal of profit until the Credit Amount has been paid out in full; and

(d) act in accordance with this Agreement and the applicable contractual and legal requirements.

§ 9 Client’s Obligations

The Client shall, in particular:

(a) maintain a trading account balance of at least EUR 2,500.00 at the time this credit request is made;

(b) request a credit of at least EUR 1,000.00 to be added to the trading account;

(c) pay out the Credit Amount in full on or before 30 September 2026, and in any event before withdrawing any profit; and

(d) provide the Manager and/or Broker with all information reasonably required to add the credit and to comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

§ 10 Final Provisions

(1) This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions, correspondence and arrangements relating thereto.

(2) Amendments and supplements to this Agreement, including this written form requirement, shall be made in writing.

(3) Should any provision of this Agreement be or become invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect. The invalid or unenforceable provision shall be replaced by a valid provision that most closely reflects the commercial intent of the Parties.

(4) This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including electronic counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument.
